Sat Sri Akal batchmate 🙏

We are collecting batch input to finalize the scoring rubric for this year's scholarship awards. Your vote becomes one weighted opinion — final rubric = batch average.

Program principles you'll be voting on:
  • Helping financially weak families above all
  • Girl-child education
  • Orphan & single-parent students
  • Punjab domicile (our home state)
  • Rewarding hard work — students who work alongside studies
  • Academic merit & clean record (no reappears)
  • Special achievements at college/state/national levels
  • Continuity for previous beneficiaries
  • Teacher / HOD recommendation
  • First-generation college students

A
FINANCIAL NEED — The Soul of the Scholarship
Q1

FAMILY INCOME — how much should annual family income weigh in scoring?

Out of a total 100 score points, this is the slice given to income-based need alone.

Q2

INCOME MEASUREMENT — how should we calculate "poor enough"?

Family of 7 earning ₹3 lakh vs family of 2 earning ₹3 lakh — very different reality.

Q3

FAMILY ASSETS CHECK — should owned property count against need?

A family may report low income but own land/shop/multiple vehicles. Should this affect scoring?

Q4

NUMBER OF DEPENDENTS — should family size matter beyond per-capita?

A family supporting 6 dependents on one earner faces deeper hardship.

B
FAMILY CIRCUMSTANCES — Hardships Beyond Income
Q5

PARENTS STATUS — how much should loss of parent(s) count?

Orphan or single-parent students carry hardships income alone can't capture.

Q6

SINGLE-PARENT / WIDOW HOUSEHOLD — mother as sole earner. Weight?

Cases where the mother alone supports the family but both parents are technically alive.

Q7

DISABILITY / CHRONIC ILLNESS — student or parent with medical disability (with certificate)

Recognized disability adds hidden costs of treatment & care.

Q8

FIRST-GENERATION COLLEGE STUDENT — neither parent finished a degree

Breaking generational barriers. New idea, gaining importance globally in admissions.

C
PRIORITY GROUPS — Gender, Geography, Domicile
Q9

GIRL CHILD — priority for girls' education. Weight?

Girls in many parts of Punjab still face higher barriers to college.

Q10

🪷 PUNJAB DOMICILE — preference for Punjab-born students?

GNDEC's roots are in Punjab. Should home-state students get a scoring boost?

Q11

RURAL BACKGROUND — how should we classify geography?

A student from a 2,000-person village faces different barriers than one from Ludhiana city.

D
ACADEMIC PERFORMANCE — Proving They Can Succeed
Q12

ACADEMIC MARKS — weight for percentage/grades?

Across last 2 semesters average. Higher = more reward.

Q13

NO BACKLOGS / NO EXAM REAPPEARS — reward students who pass every paper first try?

Clean academic record shows discipline & focus despite hardship.

Q14

ACADEMIC TREND — should improvement across semesters matter?

A student going 60% → 75% → 85% shows resilience.

E
CHARACTER & EFFORT — Beyond Marks
Q15

WORKING ALONGSIDE STUDIES — student doing part-time job to support themselves

Self-help shows character. Should we reward it?

Q16

SPECIAL AWARDS & ACHIEVEMENTS — how should we score multi-level recognition?

Student may be College Sports Captain, State Math Olympiad winner, National Hockey player, etc.

Q17

TEACHER / HOD RECOMMENDATION — how should we use the reference letter?

Faculty know student character & circumstances better than any form.

Q18

LEADERSHIP / NSS / NCC / VOLUNTEERING — student giving back to community

Class rep, NSS volunteer, sports captain, social service. Does this matter?

F
POLICY DECISIONS — Continuity, Fairness, Awards
Q19

PREVIOUS BENEFICIARY — what policy for students we already helped?

Student got our scholarship last year, applies again. Continue or rotate?

Q20

OTHER NGO / GOVT AID — student also receiving scholarship from elsewhere

Punjab Govt scholarship, another NGO, etc. Should this affect our award?

Q21

FRAUD / INCOME MISREPRESENTATION — student hid income or assets

If we discover during verification that family wealth was concealed. Action?

Q22

AWARD AMOUNT TIERS — should the amount differ by need?

Should the highest-need student get more rupees than the moderate-need one?

Q23

TOTAL SCORING SCALE — what scale should we use?

Bigger scale = more granular, fewer ties. Smaller scale = easier to communicate.

Q24

TIE-BREAKING — when two students score equal, who wins?

Inevitable in any scoring system. Pick the primary tie-breaker.

Q25

NUMBER OF ANNUAL AWARDEES — how many students should we support each year?

Trade-off between breadth (more students) and depth (bigger amounts per student).
