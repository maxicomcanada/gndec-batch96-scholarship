Always required:

  • Government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar / passport)
  • GNDEC enrollment/registration card
  • Most recent semester marks transcript
  • Father's and mother's photo ID + most recent year's income proof (salary slips, ITR, or affidavit if informal income)
