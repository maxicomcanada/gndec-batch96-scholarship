👀 Things Specifically Worth a Look

Polish + intentional design choices worth noticing.

  • Mobile experience — open any page on your phone; nav collapses to hamburger, hero stacks, all charts adapt
  • Dark mode toggle in top-right of any public page (persists across pages and reloads)
  • Hover effects — module cards lift, recipient cards reveal accent, donor tiles glow
  • Cross-module data flow — the home page impact dashboard pulls from donor + recipient localStorage in real-time
  • Admin vs public — notice the visual shift from light/welcoming to dark/operational when you cross the admin gate
  • The 5 "continuity recipients" — students who received scholarships in all 3 rounds — highlighted on home + given a +2 badge on recipients page
  • Story section on home — "It started over a WhatsApp group" narrative with the 1996 visual
