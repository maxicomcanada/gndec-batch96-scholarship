Questions before applications open?

If you're a current GNDEC student preparing in advance, gather these documents so you're ready when applications open: government photo ID, parents' income proof, your latest semester marks transcript, and any documents supporting hardship claims (orphan/widow/disability/agrarian certificates).

For Punjab connection: birth certificate(s), school transcripts, ration card, domicile certificate.

For GNDEC legacy: relative's degree certificate.
