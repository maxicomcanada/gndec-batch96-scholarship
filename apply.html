Quick check

Am I eligible?

Five quick questions to see if you should apply. No data is saved — just for your own clarity.

🎓Are you currently enrolled at Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College, Ludhiana?
💰Does paying the next semester's fee cause genuine financial difficulty for your family?
📚Are you in reasonable academic standing (not on the verge of dismissal)?
📝Can you arrange a brief recommendation from a faculty member at GNDEC?
⏱Do you have ~10 minutes to fill out the form (you can save & resume)?
Before you start

What to keep handy

You can save and return mid-way, but it's faster if you have these ready before you begin.

University Roll Number (URN)

Your GNDEC URN — the long number on your ID card / mark sheet.

Passport-size photo (optional)

JPG/PNG under 2MB. Skip if you don't have one handy — not mandatory.

Family income figure

Annual combined income from all sources (best estimate is fine).

Subject-wise marks

Marks per subject from at least your latest semester. Bulk-paste from Excel works.

A teacher's name & department

The faculty member recommending you. Email/phone if available.

Bank details (optional now)

Only required if you're shortlisted. Skip for now if you don't have them.
