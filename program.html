📋 Program Details

Who we support and
how much we award.

A transparent overview of who qualifies, what amounts you can receive, and what the journey from application to award looks like.

Eligibility

Who can apply

The scholarship is open to students currently enrolled at Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College, Ludhiana, who meet the following criteria.

🎓

Currently enrolled at GNDEC

You must be an active student in any program — B.Tech (CSE, ECE, IT, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical), B.Arch, or B.Voc — across any semester.

💔

Genuine financial need

The scholarship is need-heavy. Annual family income, parents' employment, dependents, and circumstances like loss of a parent are the primary considerations.

📚

Reasonable academic standing

Strong academics help but aren't required. A deserving student with average grades and high need will often outrank a high-scorer with no need.

📝

Teacher recommendation

One recommendation from a faculty member at GNDEC who knows your circumstances — not necessarily formal, a short note suffices.

Scholarship amounts

What you can receive

Amounts vary by your assessed need, academic semester, and the program's cycle. Below are the typical ranges based on historical awards.

Need tierTypical awardSemester coverage
Highest need ₹20,000 – ₹30,000 Full semester fee for most B.Tech / B.Voc programs
Moderate need ₹10,000 – ₹20,000 Substantial portion of semester fee
Special / Merit ₹15,000 – ₹25,000 Recognition awards for exceptional academic standing

Funds are credited directly to your GNDEC fee account. If you've already paid the current semester, the amount is adjusted against your next semester.

Process

From application to award

Four steps, fully online, fully transparent. The whole journey takes about 3–4 weeks from application open to disbursement.

  1. 1

    Application opens

    Watch this site or your GNDEC notice board for the next round announcement. Online form takes ~10 minutes; you can save mid-way and return.

  2. 2

    Online interview

    Shortlisted applicants are invited for a 15-minute online interview with members of the 1996 Batch GNDEC Alumni Committee. Conducted via video call.

  3. 3

    Scoring & decision

    The committee applies a transparent 10-point rubric (financial need, parents status, academics, gender, working alongside studies, achievements). Decisions are made collectively.

  4. 4

    Disbursement

    Awarded amounts are credited directly to your GNDEC semester fee account. You receive a confirmation email with the amount and adjustment details.

What's covered

Where the money goes

100% of every donated rupee lands in a student's tuition. We have zero overhead — the committee meets and operates entirely online, on their own time.

🎓

Semester tuition

The primary use of funds. Adjusted against your current or next semester fee at GNDEC.

📚

Lab fees & exam fees

Included in semester fee coverage at GNDEC. No separate disbursement.

🚫

NOT covered

Hostel, food, travel, books — these are personal expenses. Aim is to keep the academic path clear.

Ready to apply?

The application is online, takes about 10 minutes, and auto-saves as you go.
