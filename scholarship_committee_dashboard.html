Selection Criteria & Scoring Rubric

The total score is out of 10 points. The rubric is need-heavy: a deserving student with high need but average grades can outrank a wealthier high-scorer. Plus achievement bonus and adjustments (capped).

Financial Need4 pts
Lower income → more points. Rural: <₹1L=4, <₹3L=3, <₹6L=1.5. Urban: <₹2L=4, <₹5L=3, <₹10L=1.5.
Parents Status2 pts
Both deceased = 2, one alive = 1, both alive = 0.
Academic Performance2 pts
Avg of all marks: 90+ = 2, 75–89 = 1.5, 60–74 = 1, <60 = 0.5.
Gender (girl-child)1 pt
Female = 1 pt. Reflects girl-child education priority.
Working alongside studies1 pt
Yes = 1 pt — recognizes self-help.
Achievements / Special Mentions+0.5 (capped)
College +0.1 · State +0.25 · National +0.35 · International +0.5. Maximum bonus capped at +0.5.
Previous Recipient (us)+0.5
Continuity bonus — supporting the same student through their program.
Other NGO Aid−0.5
Fairness deduction — student already supported elsewhere.

Tie-breakers (in order): 1) total score 2) need score 3) both parents deceased 4) academic average 5) working status 6) highest achievement level 7) teacher recommendation strength.
