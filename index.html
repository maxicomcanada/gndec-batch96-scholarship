Our Story

It started over a WhatsApp group
and a quiet observation.

Around 2023, members of the 1992–1996 batch of Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College, Ludhiana started hearing the same story from juniors at their alma mater — bright students dropping out, or barely scraping through, because they simply couldn't make the next semester's fee.

A small group started chipping in privately. Then more joined. The WhatsApp group became a planning circle. The planning circle organised the first round of formal interviews in February 2024, and in March 2024 we distributed our first 65 scholarships — ₹8,12,000 handed directly into students' fee accounts.

We don't run an office. We don't take overhead. The committee meets online, conducts interviews online, and every single rupee donated reaches a student's tuition. Two more rounds have followed — January 2025 and November 2025 — supporting 98 unique students with 131 awards totalling ₹19.32 lakhs.

And we're just getting started.

How it works

Three steps, fully transparent

From application to award, every step happens in the open — no hidden criteria, no agency overhead.

1

🎓 Student Applies

Online application with academic record, family circumstances, recommendation, and a brief personal statement. ~10 minutes, auto-saved as you go.

2

⚖️ Committee Reviews

A 10-point need-heavy scoring rubric is applied: financial need, family situation, academics, gender, work alongside studies, achievements. Applicants are interviewed online.

3

💝 Award Disbursed

Funds go directly into the student's semester fee account at GNDEC. Students who've already paid have it adjusted against their next semester. Zero overhead, zero leakage.
