📋 About the Program

What is the 1996 Batch GNDEC Alumni Scholarship?

It's a need-based scholarship program started in 2024 by the 1992–1996 batch of alumni from Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College, Ludhiana. We've supported 98 unique students with 131 awards totalling ₹19.32 lakhs across three rounds (Mar 2024, Jan 2025, Nov 2025).

The program runs without a formal office or overhead — funds come from alumni donations, are disbursed by an alumni committee, and go directly into students' fee accounts at GNDEC.

Who runs this scholarship?

A volunteer committee of alumni from the 1992-1996 batch of GNDEC. Day-to-day administration is coordinated by Gulvir Singh, Assistant Professor, Mechanical Engineering Department at GNDEC.

Reach him at gulvir75@gmail.com or +91 95924-57515.

Is this an official GNDEC scholarship?

No, this is an alumni-initiated scholarship, not run by the college administration. We coordinate with the college's fee office to credit awarded amounts directly to your fee account, but the selection and funding is fully independent.

🎓 Eligibility

Who is eligible to apply?

Any student currently enrolled at Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College (GNDEC), Ludhiana in B.Tech, B.Arch, or B.Voc programs is eligible. The scholarship is open across all branches and semesters.

The primary criterion is genuine financial need — the program is need-heavy.

Do I need top grades to apply?

No. The scoring rubric is need-heavy: 4 of 10 points are for financial need, 2 for parents status, and only 2 for academics. A deserving student with high need and average grades will often outrank a high-scorer with no need.

That said, reasonable academic standing matters — we're not supporting students at risk of dismissal.

I'm a girl student. Is there a preference?

Yes. The rubric awards 1 point for girl-child applicants — a small preference reflecting our commitment to encouraging more girls in engineering.

I'm already getting another scholarship. Can I still apply?

Yes, but disclose honestly. Currently receiving aid from another NGO or government scholarship is a 0.5-point deduction in the scoring — not a disqualification. Be transparent; the committee values honesty.

I got this scholarship before. Can I apply again?

Yes! Being a previous recipient is actually a +0.5 point continuity bonus. We've supported many students across multiple semesters — 5 students have received scholarships in all 3 rounds so far.

📝 Application Process

How long does the application take?

About 10 minutes if you have your URN, family income, semester marks, and a teacher's name handy. The form auto-saves as you go — you can pause and return anytime on the same browser.

What information do I need?

Required: name, URN, gender, birth place, course/semester, family income, parents status, teacher recommender's name & department, declaration.

Recommended: subject-wise marks for at least your latest semester, family details (dependents, occupations), achievements (chip pickers help — e.g., College President, Sports Captain).

Optional: photo, bank details (only required if shortlisted).

I made a mistake. Can I edit my application after submitting?

The form runs on your browser — submissions are emailed/downloaded by you. So if you spot a mistake after submitting, simply submit a corrected version and let the committee know via email (gulvir75@gmail.com) which one to use.

Can I apply on behalf of someone else?

Yes — parents or family members often help young students with the form. Just ensure the answers reflect the student's actual circumstances, not the helper's.

Is my data confidential?

Yes. Your application is reviewed only by the alumni selection committee. We never publish details without your permission. Recipients shown publicly (on the Recipients page) show only name, year, college, branch, and amount — no personal/family financial details.

⚖️ Selection Process

How are applications evaluated?

The committee applies a transparent 10-point rubric:

• Financial Need: 4 pts (income-based)
• Parents Status: 2 pts (orphan = 2, single parent = 1)
• Academic Performance: 2 pts (90+ = 2, 75-89 = 1.5, 60-74 = 1, <60 = 0.5)
• Girl-child preference: 1 pt
• Working alongside studies: 1 pt
• Bonuses/penalties: ±0.5 for prior recipients / other NGO aid / national achievements

Tie-breakers: need score → parents → academics → working → achievement level → teacher recommendation strength.

Will I be interviewed?

Yes. Shortlisted applicants are invited for a 15-minute online interview with one or two members of the alumni committee, conducted via Zoom or Google Meet. This is informal — we mainly want to understand your circumstances and answer any questions you have.

How long until I hear back?

Typically 3-4 weeks from application close: a few days for shortlisting, a week or two for interviews, then committee deliberation, then disbursement. We always email final decisions — please check spam folders.

🎉 After You're Selected

How will I receive the money?

Awarded amounts are credited directly to your GNDEC semester fee account. If you've already paid the current semester's fee, the amount is adjusted against your next semester. You don't need to do anything besides confirming your bank details (if requested).

Do I need to repay it?

No. This is a grant, not a loan. There's nothing to repay.

Many recipients choose to pay it forward after graduating — by becoming donors themselves or by recommending the program to current students. But that's entirely voluntary.

Is there any work commitment or obligation?

No work commitment. We do appreciate if you share a brief update on your progress (a sentence or two) when we follow up — helps us track impact and improve. But this is informal, not contractual.

Can I receive this multiple semesters in a row?

Yes. Many students do — five have received it in all 3 rounds so far. You'll need to re-apply each round with updated information. Previous recipients get a +0.5 continuity bonus in scoring.

💝 For Donors

How can I donate?

Reach out to Gulvir Singh at gulvir75@gmail.com or +91 95924-57515 for bank transfer details. We accept INR, USD, and CAD contributions; we have donors from US, Canada, and India.

What's the smallest amount I can donate?

There's no minimum. Even ₹2,000 covers a meaningful portion of one student's lab fees. Many of our donors contribute ₹11,000-₹21,000 (covering one student's full semester fee in CSE/IT). Larger gifts at the Patron (₹1L+) and Founder (₹5L+) tiers help us scale.

How do I know my donation reached a student?

Every round, all recipients with names and amounts are publicly listed on the Recipients page. You can see exactly who was supported and how much each received. Transparency is the foundation of this program.

Donors at Patron and Founder tier are honored on the Honor Wall (unless you prefer to give anonymously, which we fully respect).

Is my donation tax-deductible?

This is an informal alumni initiative without 80G registration, so donations are not tax-deductible in India at this time. We're a volunteer-run program with zero overhead — every rupee reaches a student. Many donors choose to give knowing this.

Can I sponsor a specific student?

Yes. If you'd like to direct your gift to a specific recipient (e.g., from a particular branch, or someone you've met), let us know when donating. Otherwise, gifts go to the general pool and are allocated by the committee based on need.
